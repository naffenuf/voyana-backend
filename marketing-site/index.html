Walk, Listen, Discover

Your personal tour guide in your pocket

Step 1

Choose Your Adventure

Browse curated walking tours across NYC neighborhoods. Each tour includes detailed maps, historical context, and carefully selected stops. Filter by borough, duration, or distance to find the perfect walk.

  • Tours generally an hour or under
  • Average 5-7 stops per tour

Step 2

Follow Your Route

GPS tracks your location and guides you from stop to stop. The app automatically triggers audio narration when you arrive at each point of interest. Walk at your own pace—pause, skip ahead, or revisit any location.

  • Real-time position tracking
  • Auto-play when you arrive
  • Distance to next stop displayed

Step 3

Learn the Stories

Each stop includes high-quality narrated audio covering architecture, history, and local culture. Read detailed descriptions, view photos, and discover what makes each location special. Save your favorite spots to revisit later.

  • High-quality audio narration
  • Photos and historical context
  • Bookmark favorite locations

Built with Love for NYC

Voyana was created by New Yorkers who believe that every neighborhood in this city has incredible stories to tell. We've spent years exploring hidden corners, discovering forgotten histories, and uncovering the moments that make NYC truly special.

Our mission is simple: make these stories accessible to everyone. Whether you're a lifelong New Yorker or visiting for the first time, Voyana helps you experience the city like never before—through the eyes of locals who know it best.

Every tour is carefully researched with high-quality narrated audio, and designed to bring history, architecture, and culture to life. We're constantly adding new tours and improving existing ones based on feedback from our community of explorers.
